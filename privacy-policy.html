Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Kanakk Book is a bookkeeping application designed to help businesses manage customer accounts, credits, payments and reminders. This page explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information you enter

The app may allow you to store business and customer information such as names, phone numbers, addresses, notes, transaction amounts, dates and descriptions. This information is used to provide the app's bookkeeping features.

Local data

Kanakk Book may store account and transaction information locally on your device so that the app can work as intended. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and maintaining backups when available.

Backup and restore

Where backup and restore features are available, data may be handled according to the backup mechanism provided by the app and the service you choose to use.

Advertising

The free version may display advertisements. Advertising services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

Payments and premium

Premium purchases are processed through the applicable app-store billing system. Kanakk Book does not receive or store your full payment-card details through the app.

WhatsApp reminders

If you choose to send a reminder through WhatsApp, the app opens or uses the WhatsApp sharing flow to help you send the message. You control whether the message is sent.

Children's privacy

Kanakk Book is intended for business use and is not directed at children.
