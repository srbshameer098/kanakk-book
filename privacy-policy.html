Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Kanakk Book respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Kanakk Book application.

1. Information We Collect

Kanakk Book may allow you to store information such as customer names, phone numbers, transaction details, credit amounts, payment records and other information that you choose to enter into the app.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate permission to store and use information belonging to your customers.

2. How We Use Information

Information entered into Kanakk Book is used to provide the application's features, including customer management, credit tracking, payment tracking, transaction history, statements and reminders.

3. Data Storage

Kanakk Book may store information locally on your device. Certain premium features may also provide backup and restore functionality.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups of your important business information.

4. Customer Information

If you enter customer information into Kanakk Book, you should use that information only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Payment Information

Premium purchases may be processed through the applicable app store or payment provider. Kanakk Book does not directly store your complete payment card information.

6. Advertising

The free version of Kanakk Book may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services.

Premium subscriptions may provide an advertisement-free experience according to the applicable premium plan.

7. Third-Party Services

Kanakk Book may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, such as advertising, analytics, cloud backup or payment processing.

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Kanakk Book is intended for business and general productivity purposes and is not specifically directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the information provided on our Contact page .
